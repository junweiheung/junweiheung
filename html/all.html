All explainers

Every Bottleneck explainer in one place. Read them as a list, newest first, or explore them three ways: by topic, by the companies that turn up across stories, and by country.

All explainers

    Each card is a topic and lists the explainers on it; the arrows show how one topic feeds the next. Hover an explainer to light up the others it connects to. Drag to move around; pinch, or hold Ctrl or ⌘ and scroll, to zoom.

    Hover an explainer to see what it connects to: links in the text, shared companies, or a related topic.

    Who shows up where

    Each black box is a category, the same ones the explainers are grouped by. The blue companies show up in more than one of them: those are the ones holding different industries together. Click a category to pop out every company in it; click it again to close. Click a company to see where it appears.

    Category (or, inside an explainer, a value-chain step) Company Company in several explainers or steps

    Where the stories are

    Each country is shaded by the number of explainers about it, from empty to deep gold: pieces about one country’s economy or history, or about a company based there. Hover to see the count, click a shaded country to list its explainers. Scroll or pinch to zoom, drag to pan.
