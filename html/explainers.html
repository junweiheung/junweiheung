About the series

Why "Bottleneck"?

In every industry, a few players are the ones nobody can replace quickly. They're usually the ones keeping the money. Each explainer picks one industry, follows the money and the data through it, and asks who the bottleneck is.

How these work

  • Every number links to its source at the bottom of the page.
  • The charts change as you scroll, so you can read them at your own pace.
  • They're living documents and get updated when companies report.
  • They're for learning, not investment advice.
