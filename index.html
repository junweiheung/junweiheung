(About me)

I’m Jun Wei, a student deeply passionate about synergy and cross-functional paradigms — just kidding, I don’t know what half of those words mean. I’m into harmless pranks, deadpan jokes, and data.

My longest-running prank: there’s a barista who knows my order by heart. Every time I walk in, he smiles, reaches for the cup and says “Teh Peng?” And every time, I order something completely different.

He groans. He questions his life choices. Once he just stared at me in silence for a full three seconds.

He’s basically a prediction model trained on my past behaviour — and I’m the edge case that breaks it.

These days, my chaotic coffee orders are sitting in some database, quietly ruining someone’s sales dashboard. I like being on the other side of that.

What happens

We order, we tap, we pay. Somewhere a model guesses what we’ll do next. I like building the systems that make those guesses — and studying the moments they get it wrong.
