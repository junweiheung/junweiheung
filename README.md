## Hi there 👋

Hi, I’m Jun Wei, a Business AI Systems student who is deeply passionate about synergy, leveraging cross-functional paradigms and driving impactful outcomes.

Just kidding. I don’t know what half of those words mean.

The real me likes to troll around: harmless pranks, deadpan jokes, and convincing my friends of fake facts until I revealed eventually.

My longest-running prank: there’s a barista who knows my order by heart. Every time I walk in, he smiles, reaches for the cup and says _"Teh Peng?”_

And every time, I order something completely different.

He groans. He questions his life choices. Once he just stared at me in silence for a full three seconds.

Aside from making a barista question his career choices, I’m fascinated by the data hiding behind everyday habits. We order, we tap, we pay, and before you know it, my chaotic coffee orders are sitting in some database, quietly ruining someone’s sales dashboard. I like being on the other side of that: building the pipelines, cleaning up the mess, and turning all that data into something that makes sense!
